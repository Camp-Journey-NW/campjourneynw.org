Summer 2026 Wrap-Up: A Season to Remember

This summer marked another incredible chapter for Camp Journey NW. Over 200 young campers from across the Pacific Northwest gathered for a season packed with hiking, team challenges, campfire storytelling, and a record-breaking canoe race on Cascade Lake.

A heartfelt thank-you goes out to our dedicated staff, the many parent volunteers who donated their weekends, and every camper who brought their whole heart to the journey. Planning for Summer 2027 is already underway — registration information will be announced in early spring.

Photo galleries and a full program summary will be posted to the new website when it launches. In the meantime, reach out through our contact page if you have questions.

July 4, 2026 • Community

New Wilderness Skills Track Launches This Fall

We're thrilled to announce the debut of our Wilderness Skills Track — a new multi-weekend series designed for teens aged 13–17 who want to deepen their outdoor knowledge beyond the traditional camp experience.

Topics include Leave-No-Trace principles, basic wilderness first aid, navigation with map and compass, shelter building, and fire-craft safety. Sessions will be held at our Pine Ridge site starting in October 2026. Space is limited to 24 participants per cohort.

Interested? See the events page for enrollment details or email us directly.
